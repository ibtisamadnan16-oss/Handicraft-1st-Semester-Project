Our Story

Established in 1972, Venus Handicrafts originated from a humble collective of passionate master artisans who recognized that ancestral crafting techniques were rapidly disappearing amidst modern mass production. With an unwavering love for traditional artistry, our founders created a dedicated sanctuary where authentic techniques could thrive without compromise.

Over five decades, Venus Handicrafts has evolved from a local artisanal guild into a globally renowned manufacturing and exporting enterprise. From hand-forged wrought iron and chiseled brass urns to intricately carved walnut boxes and kaleidoscopic Turkish mosaic lamps, each piece embodies the dedication of artisans who pour their heart and cultural soul into every creation.

Today, our creations bring genuine warmth, timeless character, and ancestral elegance to luxury homes, five-star heritage hotels, and boutique design spaces across the globe.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower indigenous craftspeople and their families by providing sustainable, ethical livelihood opportunities while safeguarding endangered handicraft techniques for future generations.

  • Provide fair-trade wages and respectful work environments for artisan clusters.
  • Preserve ancient folk arts including brass repoussé, hand-carving, and glazed pottery.
  • Bridge traditional handcrafting with modern, eco-conscious global consumer standards.
🌟

Our Vision

Our vision is to be recognized worldwide as the foremost destination and trusted export authority for authentic, heritage-inspired handmade decorative handicrafts.

  • Elevate traditional handcrafted art to the forefront of global interior architecture.
  • Foster cross-cultural appreciation for artisanal authenticity over disposable plastic goods.
  • Continuously expand our global reach across every major continent with sustainable trade.
