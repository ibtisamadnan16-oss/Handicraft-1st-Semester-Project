Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common inquiries about our manufacturing process, international export services, wholesale partnerships, and custom artisanal designs.

Since 1972, Venus Handicrafts has specialized in a rich, comprehensive spectrum of handcrafted decorative and utility items. Our primary manufacturing categories include:

  • Metal Handicrafts: Wrought iron wall art, brass vases, brass figurines, and polished aluminium centerpieces.
  • Wood & Glass: Hand-carved solid wood panels, antique mirrors, and mouth-blown decorative glass vessels.
  • Living & Dining Decor: Elegant table wares, fruit platters, charger plates, and tabletop ornaments.
  • Lighting & Ambiance: Handcrafted candle accessories, candelabras, tea light holders, and decorative lanterns.

Explore our comprehensive collection directly on our Products Catalog.

Yes, absolutely. Exporting has been a core pillar of Venus Handicrafts for over five decades. We routinely supply our artistic handicraft collections to clients across:

  • North America & Europe: Serving leading department stores, boutique decor chains, and luxury gift shops.
  • Middle East & GCC: Fulfilling large hospitality decor projects, luxury hotels, and palatial home accents.
  • Asia-Pacific: Partnering with regional lifestyle brands and retail distribution networks.

All our overseas consignments comply with global export packaging, fumigation, moisture protection, and custom maritime or air-freight logistical protocols.

Yes, we warmly welcome wholesale and commercial bulk inquiries. We work extensively with global importers, trade wholesalers, retail catalog houses, and hospitality developers.

  • Flexible Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs): Accommodating both mixed-carton trial shipments and full 20ft / 40ft container loads (FCL).
  • Tiered Wholesale Pricing: Competitive factory-direct bulk rate cards based on order volume.
  • Custom Packaging & Barcoding: Private-label boxing, barcodes, and master carton drop-ship packaging tailored to your brand.

To request our current wholesale trade sheet, please submit your inquiries via our Contact Us page.

We pride ourselves on using 100% genuine, premium-grade, ethically sourced materials engineered for aesthetic beauty and generational durability:

✨ Solid Brass: Hand-cast and engraved with antique, mirror, or brushed patinas.
⚒️ Wrought & Cast Iron: Powder-coated and rust-treated for interior & exterior decor.
🪵 Seasoned Hardwoods: Mango wood, Sheesham (rosewood), and acacia with beeswax finish.
🏺 Glass & Aluminium: Kiln-fused artisan glass and cast alloy aluminium with nickel/gold plating.

Yes, customization is one of our greatest strengths. Our master craftsmen and design department can bring your unique design concepts to reality:

  • Custom Specifications: Dimension alterations, color finishes, electroplating choices, and engraving details.
  • Bespoke Prototyping: We develop clay, wax, or metal physical prototypes based on sketches, CAD drawings, or sample photographs.
  • Private Label Manufacturing (OEM/ODM): Exclusive contract manufacturing with complete intellectual property protection.
