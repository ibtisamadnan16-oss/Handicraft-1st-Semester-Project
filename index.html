Preserving Ancient Traditions

Authentic Heritage Handcrafted With Passion

At Venus Handicrafts, every artifact tells an enduring story of human creativity, patience, and devotion. Founded over five decades ago, our collective brings together master woodcarvers, iron smelters, glass artists, and metal sculptors dedicated to keeping centuries-old artisanal traditions alive.

Unlike mass-manufactured factory goods, our handicrafts are individually shaped by hand using sustainably sourced woods, solid brass alloys, hand-blown glassware, and authentic ceramic clays. We invite you to bring genuine cultural warmth and timeless elegance into your home.
